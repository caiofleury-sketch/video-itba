Chart definitions and evidence limits

The study is presented by scientific question rather than chronology. The underlying comparisons used different reference models and outcomes. The main chart translates the numerical AUC change into extra correctly ordered target/other customer pairs per 10,000, rounded to whole pair equivalents. This is a standard pairwise interpretation of AUC, with ties receiving half credit, not additional detected customers or pesos. For relative funds the reference probability is 89.5959% and the new probability is 89.7415%: a modest +0.1456 percentage-point improvement. Raw AUC differences remain in the evidence table below. Profit consistency records whether a feature addition improved both inspected historical months versus snapshot plus three-month relative funds. These are separate measurement columns, not a common score or global feature ranking.

“—” means no comparable verified result was supplied for that measurement. It is not zero or failure.
