We added five feature packages to help predict Premium exit. The question was simple: does a customer's recent behavior tell us more than their current values alone?

Lags preserve last month. Changes show movement. Averages estimate the usual level. Relative balance and payroll measure change against each customer's own history. Card utilization compares balances with available limits.

We retrained each package against the same repaired baseline, holding learner settings and training rules fixed across five matched seeds. Only relative funds passed our selection rule: positive mean AUC improvement and at least four seed wins.

That added four columns: two relative changes and two near-zero flags. After twenty tuning evaluations, the final five-seed ensemble scored about four hundred and two on Inicial.

We then explored longer funds history, usage changes, monetary ranks, and EMA. Funds history and usage improved profit in both historical months. EMA was mixed. None of these pilot variants was publicly scored.

Relative balance and payroll were our strongest controlled-screen addition. Longer funds history and usage remain candidates for further testing. The final public score belongs to the complete recipe, not to any feature alone.
